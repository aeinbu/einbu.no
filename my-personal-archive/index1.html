How it works

  1. Get it in. Upload, drag-and-drop, email it in, or scan with your phone. Bulk import from a folder if you're bringing years of history.
  2. We make sense of it. Documents are read, dated, categorised and tagged automatically. Search by any word on the page — or by what you remember about it.
  3. Find it in seconds. Type "boiler warranty" or "2023 tax" and it's there. Share with a partner or family member when you need to.

Option B

Self-hosted

Your server. Your data. Nothing leaves your house.

Run the whole thing on your own hardware — a home server, a NAS, a VPS, a Raspberry Pi. Your documents never touch our infrastructure, because there isn't any. You keep the database, the files and the backups.

One command to install. Full API, Docker image, and a documented data format you can read without us.

Best for: the technically comfortable, the privacy-first, and anyone who wants their family's records to outlive any company.

Questions, answered

Is my data encrypted?

Yes — in transit and at rest. On self-hosted installs, encryption keys never leave your machine.

Can I import what I already have?

Yes. Drag in folders, bulk-upload, or forward emails. Most people move years of paperwork in an afternoon.

What if I want to leave?

Export everything in one click. Your documents, your metadata, your structure — in open formats.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Scan a document with your camera and it's filed before you've put the kettle on.

Do I need to be technical?

Only if you choose self-hosting. The managed option is genuinely a five-minute setup.
